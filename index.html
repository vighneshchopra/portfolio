Enterprise Client Onboarding

Driving Clinical SaaS Adoption Through Role-Tailored Onboarding Pathways

Context & The Challenge

Following high-stakes clinical software rollouts, medical personnel (hospital administrators, front-desk billing staff, and consulting doctors) faced high cognitive load. Generic training sessions led to panic tickets, appointment schedule bottlenecks, and adoption friction.

Execution & Methodology

  • Role-Segmented Modules: Replaced monolithic 2-hour overviews with 20-minute targeted drills (billing/intake workflows for front-desk; charting & e-prescriptions for doctors).
  • High-Touch Live Walkthroughs: Led independent daily interactive screen-share sessions in a mirrored staging environment, tackling real clinic patient cases live.
  • Visual Job Aids: Designed concise step-by-step visual cheat sheets placed at workstations, eliminating the need to read dense PDF handbooks.

Operational Triage & Incident Control

Resolving Urgent "System Outages" Through Independent Root-Cause Analysis

Context & The Challenge

During peak operating hours, a high-volume outpatient facility escalated that their billing and discharge pipeline had crashed. The client panicked and demanded immediate engineering intervention, threatening to disrupt an active product sprint.

Execution & Methodology

  • Rapid De-escalation: Jumped on a live session within minutes to calm the billing director and obtain exact timestamps and user session IDs.
  • Methodical Reproduction: Replicated the exact patient record state in staging. Verified that backend services, database transactions, and network status were operating normally.
  • Root-Cause Pinpointed: Discovered an unselected regional tariff dropdown caused by a confusing UI toggle default, blocking validation without an explicit error banner.
  • Client Walkthrough & Product Ticket: Guided the clinic manager live to complete the billing flow, then filed a concise UI bug ticket with reproduction steps to improve error-handling banners.

Account Expansion & Requirements Engineering

Converting Client Operational Needs into Commercial Custom Feature Deployments

Context & The Challenge

A multi-center hospital group required custom laboratory export integration and branch aggregation not present in the base software. The client struggled to formulate technical requirements, risking misalignment, scope creep, and stalled discussions.

Execution & Methodology

  • Discovery & Needs Mapping: Led discovery sessions with medical directors to isolate true operational necessities from cosmetic preferences.
  • Requirements Translation: Synthesized clinical requests into structured functional specifications for backend engineers (data flow, input/output structures, constraints).
  • Cost Scoping & Management Alignment: Collaborated with management to estimate development effort, establish delivery milestones, and present transparent costing to the client.
  • User Acceptance Testing (UAT): Validated intermediate dev builds against client workflow before conducting formal training and production deployment.
